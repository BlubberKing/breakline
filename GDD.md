## 1. Vision
- Arcade "supreme" gunplay in the spirit of **The Finals** and **Rivals** — fast, readable, not realistic.
- Hits must *feel* great: damage numbers popping to the side, hitmarkers, kill confirms, elimination banner.
- High polish bar on gun feel, movement and UI.
- **Roles:** AI = coder + UI designer. Owner = Studio work, map building, playtesting, publishing.

## 2. Game Modes

### 2.1 Main mode — Wall Push (locked design)
- **3v3.**
- A **middle wall** splits the map. Players can walk **and** shoot through it.
- Each team has a **spawn + finish line** at its end of the map.
- **3 holes per side** (6 total).
- **Boxes** spawn on the map. Players carry boxes and plug them into **their own side's** holes.
- The team with **more plugged boxes pushes the wall toward the enemy**. **Tie = wall stays still.**
- Enemies can **shoot plugged boxes to break them**.
- A team **loses when the wall reaches its finish line**.

**Built (v2, owner-specified, Oct 25):**
- **Layout:** 3v3 (Red vs Blue). Each team spawns on its own **finish line** at its end of the map (3 **invisible** spawn pads on the line). The wall starts in the middle.
- **The code only builds the gameplay pieces. You build and decorate the map.** `MatchServer` creates: invisible boundary walls + ceiling, the 2 finish lines, the 6 invisible team spawns, the middle wall with its holes, and the boxes. Nothing else (no floor, no cover, no lobby floor).
- **MapBounds (how the code fits your map):** put an **anchored, fully transparent Part named `MapBounds`** in Workspace, sized to cover the playable area. Width = X size, Length = Z size, Height = Y size; the **bottom** of the part is the ground level. Red's end is the Front (-Z) end, Blue's the Back (+Z) end (`MAP.SwapEnds` flips this). Rotating MapBounds rotates everything. Finish lines/spawns sit `MAP.FinishInset` studs in from each end; the wall is as tall as MapBounds (max `WallHeightMax`); hole + spawn positions are fractions of the width. Resize MapBounds and everything re-fits on the next server start. If it is missing, a 140x60x260 default is created at the origin and a warning is printed.
- **Lobby:** put a `SpawnLocation` named `LobbySpawn` in Workspace (anywhere); if none exists an invisible one is created inside MapBounds.
- **Wall:** transparent (plain transparent plastic, NOT Glass material, so box faces show through); players **and bullets pass through** it. **3 indents (holes) per side, 6 total.**
- **Boxes:** look like little robots: dark steel crate, glowing edges in amber (turns **team-colored when plugged**), antenna bulb, soft light, and an **animated face on all 4 sides** (blinks; wide-eyed when carried; squeezed eyes + "O" mouth when thrown; happy when it goes in; worried when worn down or drained; panicking under 30% health). **1 box spawns on each side at round start, then 1 new box per side every 60s.** Loose boxes glow yellow. **No expiry timer.**
- **Pick up:** hold **E** (0.25s) on a loose box. **Plugging:** carry a box up to one of **your own side's** empty holes (within 7 studs) and it **floats into the hole** with a short animation + burst of sparks.
- **Stealing:** you may plug **any** box in **any** hole on your side - boxes don't belong to a specific hole. You can also **extract an enemy's plugged box**: stand on their side and **hold E (1.5s)** on it, then carry it to your side and plug it into one of your holes.
- **Plugged box health:** **5000 HP.** Enemies **shoot** plugged boxes to break them (your own team can't). A **health bar + number floats over each plugged box and is visible from either side of the wall (through the wall too).**
- **Push drain:** the team that is **currently pushing the wall** (more plugged boxes) has **all its plugged boxes lose 10 HP every second** (`BOX.PushDrainPerSec`). The bar outline turns orange while a box is draining. Tie = nobody drains.
- **Carrying:** holding a box = **no guns, no gadgets** (can't shoot/aim/reload), slightly slower. **Two-handed pose:** in **first person** a small box (smaller + lower than before so it doesn't block the screen, `CARRY_VM` in MatchConfig) is held in both hands with a bob and a throw thrust; in **third person** (what others see) the gun disappears and **both hands (IK) hold the box**, torso leans less. **Left click = throw** (decent distance), **Q = drop**.
- **Catching + throwing in:** a thrown box that gets within 6 studs of a **teammate** is caught mid-air. A thrown box that gets within 7 studs of an empty hole on its team's side floats in. Boxes pass through players so they never block anyone.
- **Pushing:** the team with **more plugged boxes pushes the wall toward the enemy**. Speed depends on the box lead (`MatchConfig.WALL_SPEED`): **1 box ahead 0.8 studs/s, 2 ahead 1.5, 3 ahead 2.1** (slowed from 1.5/3.0/4.5). **Tie = wall stays still.** The wall reaching a finish line wins the round for the other team.
- **Rounds (v3.5 flow, see 2.1b):** Waiting (loading screen) -> Pregame 20s -> Warp 9s -> Cards ~13s (v4.3) -> Countdown 3s -> Playing -> SlowMo 2.6s -> Result 5s -> Summary (souls screen). Teams are shuffled each round (or come from the lobby roster), extra players (7+) wait in the lobby. 5s respawn at your team's finish line.
- **Fixed:** respawning/teleporting at round start left a copy of your first-person arms + gun in the camera. Now the old viewmodel is destroyed when its character is gone and `MatchClient` sweeps any leftover every 0.5s.
- All numbers are in `MatchConfig` (ReplicatedStorage).

**Souls formula** (`MatchConfig.REWARD`, computed on the server in `MatchServer.buildReward`, the client only displays it): `Base 50` x set multiplier (**Win 1.5 / Loss 0.75 / Draw 1.0**) x kills (`1 + 0.10 per kill`) x boxes plugged (`1 + 0.05 each`) x boxes destroyed (`1 + 0.08 each`) x damage (`1 + 0.02 per full 1000 damage` dealt to players). Each multiplier is rounded to 2 decimals before it is multiplied in, so what is shown multiplies to exactly the total. Souls go into the profile (`Souls`), Wins/Losses are counted as before. `PlaceConfig.SOULS_WIN / SOULS_LOSS` are no longer used.
**Stats** are Player attributes, reset every round by `MatchServer`: `StatKills` + `StatDamage` (added by `GunServer`, players only, damage capped at the target's remaining health), `StatPlugged` (whoever's carry/throw plugged the box) and `StatDestroyed` (last shooter when a plugged box is shot to 0; drained boxes give nobody credit).
**Mouse:** the game locks first person, so the interactive screens (Pregame, Summary) contain an invisible `Modal` button, which frees the cursor while they are open.

### 2.2 Standard modes
- **1v1** is built (section 2.2b, selectable in the lobby). **2v2** rules are still TBD.

### 2.2b 1v1 mode (v4.1)
- **How the mode is picked:** the `MapBounds` part gets a **child** that says which mode the map is: any instance (Folder, Part, StringValue, TextLabel...) **named `Breakline` or `1v1`**, or a StringValue / TextLabel inside it whose Value / Text is `Breakline` / `1v1`, or the attribute `Mode` on MapBounds. No child = Breakline. (Also accepted: `OneVOne`, `1on1`, `Duel`, `WallPush`.) `MatchServer` reads it at start-up and prints `[MatchServer] mode: ...`; it is published as `MatchState.Mode`.
- **Rules:** one player per team (Red vs Blue; extra players wait in the lobby team), a pure gunfight round: **first to 5 kills wins the round** (the finish lines + spawns are still built so your map keeps working) (`MatchConfig.V1.KillsToWin`). Kills are read from the existing `StatKills` (guns, frag, sentry...). `MinPlayers` 1 so you can test alone (set 2 for live). After a death you are out `V1.RespawnTime` 4 s and **spectate your opponent**. Same flow, cutscenes and souls screen as Breakline (the souls breakdown only lists the rows that apply to a 1v1). The top bar shows each side's kills and a bar that leans toward the leader; the timer label says `RED - FIRST TO 5`.
- **Lobby side (v4.2, selectable now):** the GAME MODES tab has a **1V1** entry next to BREAKLINE (see 5.5). The PLAY block follows the selected mode (`PLAY  //  1V1` or `3V3`). `LobbyServer` keeps **one queue per mode** (`MODE_SIZE = { breakline = PlaceConfig.TEAM_SIZE, ["1v1"] = 1 }`), so a 1v1 packs two solo players and a Breakline queue packs 3v3 as before. The queue call sends the mode id (`QueueR:FireServer(queued, modeId)`), the server validates it, and `MatchRoster` carries the mode in the roster so the Breakline place knows which mode was queued. **1v1 is solo only:** a party member who tries to queue it gets a notice to leave the party first. **Both places must be updated together** (the lobby sends the mode, Breakline reads it).
- **Lobby wording for 1v1** (card text in `MODES` in `LobbyClient`): tag `DUEL // FIRST TO 5`; description: one player against one player, win gunfights, use your gadgets, first to 5 kills, sit out a few seconds after a death and watch your opponent; facts: Red vs Blue one player each / First to 5 kills wins the round / Pure gunfights: aim, gadgets, positioning / Solo only: leave your party to queue. **Style rule from the owner:** do not describe 1v1 by what it lacks (no "no boxes / no souls" lines). Breakline and 1v1 are two separate modes; players can infer the differences.

### 2.3 Death, souls and respawn (v4.1, scripts `DeathServer`, `DeathScreen`, `SoulClient`, `RespawnReveal`, `RevealFX`)
- **Roblox auto respawn is off** (`Players.RespawnTime` 600). `DeathServer` owns the timers and calls `LoadCharacter`.
- **Dead for 15 s** (`DEAD_TIME`) and you **spectate a living teammate** (first person through their eyes, click / A / D to switch). **Team wipe:** if nobody on your team is alive, **everybody on the team is reset to 10 s** (`WIPE_TIME`, e.g. you had 7 s left and spectated the last mate, he dies: everyone is now at 10) and you **free roam** (WASD fly, SPACE / C up / down, SHIFT fast) - walls stop you (sphere cast that slides along surfaces) and the camera is clamped inside `MapBounds` (the invisible boundary walls cannot be ray-cast, so MapBounds is used). Spectating your last teammate and he dies = straight into free roam.
- **Death bar (bottom of the screen):** one solid team-coloured (darkened) bar, 78 px tall: **big countdown on the left**, **name of who you spectate (or FREE ROAM) in the middle**, and the **very bottom edge is a line that shrinks** to match the time left. While a teammate revives you the middle says `BEING REVIVED BY <name> 54%`.
- **Souls:** when you die with a teammate alive a **soul drops on your body**: **v4.2: a tiny, ghostly, translucent soul** (small ghost body with two eyes and a fading wispy tail, team-coloured glow; the rings, orbiting sparks and light beam were removed) hovering low over the body (hover height 1.5, was 2.5) with a **name tag** (`NAME` / `HOLD F REVIVE   E CARRY`). Teammates: **hold E (0.3 s) = pick it up** and carry it like a box (**left click = throw, Q = drop**, guns away); **v4.2: the carried soul is small and sits by your shoulder** (`SoulClient`, `DeathServer`), or **hold F for 8 s = revive** (`REZ_TIME`). Your own soul and enemies cannot use it; it disappears the moment you respawn (timer or revive). Souls are not created on a team wipe, and all souls vanish when the round ends.
- **Revive animation:** the reviver **kneels and is rooted** (arms pressed down) next to the soul; **the revived player's body is built out of souls right there, slowly over the 8 s** (their skin, the same soul-build effect as a respawn) with a progress bar + percent on the soul's tag. **Cancel (let go / walk away / die) and the half-built body fades away**. When it completes you appear **at the soul** with only the last ~14% of the reveal left (`REZ_FINISH`), then dive into your body.
- Gadgets / guns are blocked for the carrier and the reviver. Attributes: Player `IsDead`, `DeathEnds`, `DeathTotal`, `DeathMode` (`spectate` / `roam`), `CarryingSoul`; Character `Reviving`; soul part `OwnerId`, `OwnerName`, `TeamName`, `State`, `RezBy`, `RezStart`, `RezTime`, `Ground`, `Carrier`.

## 3. Loadout

### 3.1 Active loadout (chosen before the match)
- **1 gun**
- **3 gadgets**
- **1 special movement ability**

### 3.2 Reserve
- Stores **1 extra gun + 3 extra gadgets**.
- Players can **swap between the active loadout and the reserve while dead** (respawn screen).
- Movement ability is not in the reserve (TBD whether it can be swapped mid-match).

### 3.3 Special movement abilities (v3.8: REAL, one per loadout, MOVE slot)
Everyone keeps the base kit (sprint, crouch, slide, slide-jump, mantle). The MOVE slot holds ONE of these (script: `AbilityClient`, a small icon + cooldown / bar HUD sits bottom middle; all numbers in its `CFG`):
- **Double Jump** (icon: two chevrons): press SPACE again in the air for a second jump (6.8 studs). **Cooldown 3.5s.**
- **Mega Jump** (icon: big arrow over a coil): HOLD SPACE on the ground, a **charge bar** shows bottom middle (1.3s to full), release = launch 16 to 54 studs depending on the charge. A quick tap is a normal jump. You walk at half speed while charging. **Cooldown 6s.**

### 5.4d SKIN QUALITY STANDARD (v5.4, owner Nov 4: "all skins made are on a certain level of quality so they can be sold in passes and stuff, and similar-ish to each other build wise")
Every skin - free, shop, battle pass, bundle - must pass this list before it ships. OPERATOR + SOULBOUND v9 are the reference skins: a new skin should look like it came from the same studio.
- **A. Same body, same kit.** Built only from the block kit (`K.head`, `K.core` with `K.BEVEL`, `K.band`, `K.hand`, `K.boot`, `K.fillers`) on `Skins.Proportions`. Never change body proportions, hit boxes or the slim glove size. **Fair play:** a skin may not add more than about 0.35 studs of bulk outside the body (helmets, hoods, pauldrons, packs), may not hide the body outline, and may not be near-invisible against the maps (overall brightness of the outfit must stay readable; no all-black / all-foliage outfits).
- **B. One identity, readable small.** A one-line identity (e.g. "a clean tactical operator", "a pallbearer"). One clear **read on the head** (helmet / hood / mask), **one on the torso** (carrier / coat / bandolier) and **one on the legs** (holster / tails / wraps). Test: as a black silhouette and as a 128 px thumbnail the skin is still recognisable next to the others.
- **C. Shape rules.** (1) Big / medium / small: about 60 % big shapes (body, plates, coat), 30 % medium (pouches, pads, pauldrons), 10 % small (tabs, buttons, laces); no clusters of tiny bits, nothing smaller than 0.03 studs. (2) Detail thickness only in steps: seam **0.03**, strap **0.06**, plate / pouch **0.12** studs (x `Z.u`). (3) Every detail sits **flush** on the surface under it: touching, never floating, never sunk more than half its thickness. (4) **Rotations:** axis aligned or 45 degrees. Allowed extras: ONE diagonal strap angle per skin (all diagonal straps share it) and ONE "flow tilt" of at most 12 degrees for hanging cloth / armour (coat tails, pauldron lames). No random tilts. (5) Blocks + wedges only on the body (no balls / cylinders), hero props may use cylinders. (6) Symmetric base + **1 to 3 deliberate asymmetric features** (OPERATOR: arm patch left, holster right, radio left; SOULBOUND: pauldron left, lantern right).
- **D. Colour + material.** (1) At most **3 main colours + 1 accent** + skin / bone neutral + black for straps; each main colour gets exactly one darker shade, nothing in between (no near-miss shades that look like a mistake). (2) Every main colour appears in **at least two body zones** (head / torso / legs) so the outfit ties together. (3) The accent appears in **at most 3 places**, always on purpose (brand marks, eyes, a hero detail). Breakline red is for the Breakline brand only. (4) Materials: Fabric cloth, Leather belts / holsters, Metal hardware, SmoothPlastic skin / plates / boots, Glass lenses; **Neon only for soul / premium glow, at most 2 glow sources, never team colours** (team = outlines).
- **E. Gun + hands.** The menu figure must hold **all 10 guns** with 0 hand gap in every idle pose, nothing through chest or head, and **at least 50 % of a rifle visible** from the front menu camera (offline check). Outfit pieces on the arms / hands must not cover the gun (no big gauntlets over the gloves).
- **F. Technical budget + QC.** 110 to 160 parts including hands (hard limit 180). Offline QC (AI sandbox) must pass: one connected group (nothing floating), no visible coplanar faces in two colours (flicker), feet on the ground, figure check with every gun. Then Studio screenshots: front / back / 3-4, first person with a gun, lobby skin catalog card, squad / party stage, in-match third person. **Owner sign-off** before it goes on sale.
- **G. Shop data.** Name = one word in caps, a short sub-line, a description of at most about 110 characters, card colour, the full colour table (old keys `Suit, Under, Armor, Trim, Accent, Plate, Cloth, Glove, Brand, White` included).
- **H. Tiers (PROPOSAL, not approved):** Standard = recolour / variant of an existing outfit; Rare = new outfit pieces on an existing silhouette; Epic = new head + torso silhouette + a hero prop; Legendary / Soul = everything in Epic + a glow (rule D4) + a unique hero prop. ## 8. Movement (base kit, everyone has it)
Goal: fast-paced and fluid. All speeds are multiplied by the gun's move-speed (7.4).
- **Walk 16 / sprint 30 / crouch 9** (sprint was 24). Speeds live at the top of `GunClient`.
- **Sprint:** hold Shift. **Mobile:** "Sprint" button above the jump button; **gamepad:** click the left stick. Both are tap-to-toggle
  (button turns accent-colored while on) and switch off when you stop moving. Sent to `Movement` as the `SprintToggle` attribute.
- **Jump (momentum):** low and snappy, not springy — jump height 4.6 (Roblox default 7.2) and 1.4× gravity on the way down only.
  You keep your run speed in the air, so a sprint-jump goes far and flat (+2.5 forward speed when jumping at ≥ 24 speed).
  Air control: steer slowly (turn rate 3.2 rad/s), pressing backwards brakes, no input = keep drifting. Hitting a wall drops the
  momentum into it. Walking off a ledge works the same way. All in `CFG` at the top of `Movement` (JUMP_HEIGHT, FALL_GRAVITY_MULT, AIR_*).
- **Crouch:** C / LeftCtrl / gamepad B / mobile button while **not** sprinting. Hold to crouch (`CROUCH_TOGGLE` in `Movement` switches
  to tap-toggle). Lowers hitbox + camera, can't sprint while crouched, pressing sprint stands you up. Stays crouched under low ceilings.
  Replicated as the `Crouching` character attribute (third person shows bent legs + lean).
- **Slide:** same key while sprinting (speed ≥ 20). Small start boost (+6), steering, lowered hitbox + camera, buffered input
  (press just before landing or while still speeding up). **Momentum rules:**
  - Flat ground (under ~3.5°): keeps **all** momentum for ~0.95s, then blends smoothly back into a run (or crouch if C is still held).
  - Downhill: **gains** speed (scaled by steepness, cap 58) and the slide timer pauses, so long ramps = long fast slides.
  - Uphill: **loses** speed slowly (scaled by steepness); ends early if it drops below 14.
  - Sliding off a ledge keeps the momentum into the air.
- **Slide-jump:** keeps 95% of momentum (also works in the short blend right after a slide ends).
- **Mantle:** jump into a ledge (1.4–7.5 studs). With the lower jump, mantle is how you get onto anything above ~4.5 studs.
- **First person:** your own body + gear are always hidden locally (fixes seeing your body while falling).
- **Speed feel (subtle, tunable in `SPEEDFX` at the top of `GunClient`):** sprint FOV 82 (was 80), slide FOV +7, extra FOV when going
  faster than a sprint (downhill slides); quick FOV kick + dip when a slide starts; light camera rumble while sliding; faint speed lines
  at the screen edges — start at ~17 speed, about 60% strength at a normal sprint, full at ~38 (slides) — plus a soft dark edge
  vignette at speed; all hidden while aiming. Optional looping wind sound (`Wind` in SoundBank — empty = silent).

- **Animations (v4.1, `MoveAnim`, new LocalScript in both games):** real **walk, run (sprint), crouch (idle + shuffle) and slide** animations for every player, driven procedurally on the R15 joints after the Animator (no animation assets). Walk = small stride + arm swing; run = big stride with high knees, forward lean, pumping bent arms, steady head; crouch = deep knee bend, hunched torso, low shuffle; **slide = leaning back, front leg out straight, back leg folded under, arms out, head kept level, tiny speed vibration** (the old slide leaned the whole body forward and looked broken - `ThirdPersonClient` now leans back and its old leg poses are disabled). While holding a gun the arms stay with the gun IK; with no gun (lobby), a gadget, a soul, a revive or a box the arms are animated here. Your own crouch / slide / sprint is sent with the new remote `AbilityRemotes.Move` (`AbilityServer`) as character attributes `MvCrouch` / `MvSlide` / `MvSprint` so other clients see it.
- **Movement abilities (v3.8):** see section 3.3. `Movement` stands down (releases the slide / mantle / crouch) while `AbilityMode` is set; the abilities are only active when the matching one is in your MOVE slot.

## 9. Tech Principles
- **Data-driven:** stats in config modules (WeaponConfig, etc.).
- **Server-authoritative** for damage, ammo, fire rate; client does visuals and instant feedback.
- Damage numbers are shown from the **server-confirmed** value (HitConfirm).
- Parts players and bullets should pass through (e.g. the middle wall) need **CanCollide = false AND CanQuery = false**.
- **Wall Push map:** you build the map; `MatchServer` builds the gameplay pieces into `workspace.WallPush` at server start, fitted to your **`MapBounds`** part (anchored, transparent, bottom = ground; see 2.1). Tuning for fitting is in `MatchConfig.MAP`.
- **Box look:** `BoxModel` (ReplicatedStorage) builds the box and animates the faces from box attributes (`State`, `Health`, `MaxHealth`, `PlugTeam`, `Draining`); `MatchClient` runs it.
- **Carrying:** Player attribute `Carrying` (bool). Any new gun, gadget or ability script **must refuse to work while it is true** (GunClient + GunServer already do).
- **Box damage:** `GunServer` validates a shot on a plugged box and fires the `ServerStorage.BoxDamage` BindableEvent (part, damage, shooter); `MatchServer` applies it.
