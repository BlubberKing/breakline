## 1. Vision
- Arcade "supreme" gunplay in the spirit of **The Finals** and **Rivals** — fast, readable, not realistic.
- Hits must *feel* great: damage numbers popping to the side, hitmarkers, kill confirms, elimination banner.
- High polish bar on gun feel, movement and UI.
- **Roles:** AI = coder + UI designer. Owner = Studio work, map building, playtesting, publishing.

## 2. Game Modes

### 2.1 Main mode — Wall Push (locked design)
- **3v3.**
- A **middle wall** splits the map. Players can walk **and** shoot through it.
- Each team has a **spawn + finish line** at its end of the map.
- **3 holes per side** (6 total).
- **Boxes** spawn on the map. Players carry boxes and plug them into **their own side's** holes.
- The team with **more plugged boxes pushes the wall toward the enemy**. **Tie = wall stays still.**
- Enemies can **shoot plugged boxes to break them**.
- A team **loses when the wall reaches its finish line**.

**Built (v2, owner-specified, Oct 25):**
- **Layout:** 3v3 (Red vs Blue). Each team spawns on its own **finish line** at its end of the map (3 **invisible** spawn pads on the line). The wall starts in the middle.
- **The code only builds the gameplay pieces. You build and decorate the map.** `MatchServer` creates: invisible boundary walls + ceiling, the 2 finish lines, the 6 invisible team spawns, the middle wall with its holes, and the boxes. Nothing else (no floor, no cover, no lobby floor).
- **MapBounds (how the code fits your map):** put an **anchored, fully transparent Part named `MapBounds`** in Workspace, sized to cover the playable area. Width = X size, Length = Z size, Height = Y size; the **bottom** of the part is the ground level. Red's end is the Front (-Z) end, Blue's the Back (+Z) end (`MAP.SwapEnds` flips this). Rotating MapBounds rotates everything. Finish lines/spawns sit `MAP.FinishInset` studs in from each end; the wall is as tall as MapBounds (max `WallHeightMax`); hole + spawn positions are fractions of the width. Resize MapBounds and everything re-fits on the next server start. If it is missing, a 140x60x260 default is created at the origin and a warning is printed.
- **Lobby:** put a `SpawnLocation` named `LobbySpawn` in Workspace (anywhere); if none exists an invisible one is created inside MapBounds.
- **Wall:** transparent (plain transparent plastic, NOT Glass material, so box faces show through); players **and bullets pass through** it. **3 indents (holes) per side, 6 total.**
- **Boxes:** look like little robots: dark steel crate, glowing edges in amber (turns **team-colored when plugged**), antenna bulb, soft light, and an **animated face on all 4 sides** (blinks; wide-eyed when carried; squeezed eyes + "O" mouth when thrown; happy when it goes in; worried when worn down or drained; panicking under 30% health). **1 box spawns on each side at round start, then 1 new box per side every 60s.** Loose boxes glow yellow. **No expiry timer.**
- **Pick up:** hold **E** (0.25s) on a loose box. **Plugging:** carry a box up to one of **your own side's** empty holes (within 7 studs) and it **floats into the hole** with a short animation + burst of sparks.
- **Stealing:** you may plug **any** box in **any** hole on your side - boxes don't belong to a specific hole. You can also **extract an enemy's plugged box**: stand on their side and **hold E (1.5s)** on it, then carry it to your side and plug it into one of your holes.
- **Plugged box health:** **5000 HP.** Enemies **shoot** plugged boxes to break them (your own team can't). A **health bar + number floats over each plugged box and is visible from either side of the wall (through the wall too).**
- **Push drain:** the team that is **currently pushing the wall** (more plugged boxes) has **all its plugged boxes lose 10 HP every second** (`BOX.PushDrainPerSec`). The bar outline turns orange while a box is draining. Tie = nobody drains.
- **Carrying:** holding a box = **no guns, no gadgets** (can't shoot/aim/reload), slightly slower. **Two-handed pose:** in **first person** a small box (smaller + lower than before so it doesn't block the screen, `CARRY_VM` in MatchConfig) is held in both hands with a bob and a throw thrust; in **third person** (what others see) the gun disappears and **both hands (IK) hold the box**, torso leans less. **Left click = throw** (decent distance), **Q = drop**.
- **Catching + throwing in:** a thrown box that gets within 6 studs of a **teammate** is caught mid-air. A thrown box that gets within 7 studs of an empty hole on its team's side floats in. Boxes pass through players so they never block anyone.
- **Pushing:** the team with **more plugged boxes pushes the wall toward the enemy**. Speed depends on the box lead (`MatchConfig.WALL_SPEED`): **1 box ahead 0.8 studs/s, 2 ahead 1.5, 3 ahead 2.1** (slowed from 1.5/3.0/4.5). **Tie = wall stays still.** The wall reaching a finish line wins the round for the other team.
- **Rounds (v3.5 flow, see 2.1b):** Waiting (loading screen) -> Pregame 20s -> Warp 9s -> Countdown 3s -> Playing -> SlowMo 2.6s -> Result 5s -> Summary (souls screen). Teams are shuffled each round (or come from the lobby roster), extra players (7+) wait in the lobby. 5s respawn at your team's finish line.
- **Fixed:** respawning/teleporting at round start left a copy of your first-person arms + gun in the camera. Now the old viewmodel is destroyed when its character is gone and `MatchClient` sweeps any leftover every 0.5s.
- All numbers are in `MatchConfig` (ReplicatedStorage).

### 2.1b Match flow + screens (built v3.5, all in `MatchFlow` + `LoadingScreen`)
`MatchServer` drives `ReplicatedStorage.MatchState.Phase`; every screen is built on the client from that phase and its `PhaseEnds` time.
| Phase | Length | What the player sees |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting** | until everyone loaded (max `READY_TIMEOUT` 40s, + `ROSTER_WAIT` in a real match) | **Loading screen** (`LoadingScreen`, ReplicatedFirst): black, the **v3.7 logo** big in the middle: BREAK flies in from the left, LINE from the right, they meet and the slim red slash draws in with light flowing down it (no explosions); on exit the slash flares and the words fly apart. One line of text under it: LOADING / CONNECTING / LOADING MAP / LOADING PLAYER / WAITING FOR PLAYERS 3 / 6 / ALL PLAYERS LOADED, plus a thin progress line. The client tells the server it is loaded with `MatchRemotes.ClientReady`. |
| **Pregame** | `PREGAME_TIME` 20s | You (middle) + teammates (left/right) stand side by side on glowing discs, names above. **Loadout dock at the bottom** (MOVE, PRIMARY, 3 GADGETS, 3 RESERVE; click a tile, pick an item; changes are validated and saved by the server via `PregameLoadout`, the gun you pick is the one you play with). **Clock at the top**. **Last 5s (v3.7, louder):** the scene darkens, MATCH STARTS IN appears, and a **huge number slams in every second** over the squad (white 5-4, team color 3-2-1) with a shock ring, red pulses at the screen edges and the tick sound; the clock flashes red too. The picker closes at 3. |
| **Warp** | `WARP_TIME` 9s | Fade to black -> **your soul lights up** in the black and flies (camera above and behind it, trail, dust for speed) toward a **small lit white door** far ahead -> **white flash** -> the **real map** in **first person (v3.7)**: the camera is your eyes and you **sprint onto your spawn spot facing the middle wall** (forward is read from the `Wall` part; head bob, your gun in the sprint pose via the client-only `CutsceneRun` attribute that `GunClient` reads), slowing down onto the spot; teammates (local-only figures with their skins + guns) sprint in beside you a step ahead, with light beams + landing rings. The run starts up to 30 studs behind the spot but stops short of any visible solid part behind the line, so the camera never goes through your map. -> soft flash -> normal first person. The real characters are respawned on their spawn pads and **anchored** (frozen) by the server the whole time. |
| **Countdown** | `COUNTDOWN_TIME` 3s | Big 3 - 2 - 1 on the real map, everybody frozen. Then **GO**. |
| **Playing** | until the wall reaches a finish line | The normal match. Guns are server-blocked in every phase except Playing / Result. **v3.8 respawn reveal:** when you respawn mid-round (`MatchConfig.RESPAWN_TIME` 5s) you are frozen + protected (ForceField) for `REVEAL_TIME` 1.4s while your body is **revealed from the feet up**: ~16 blue souls (the death-soul look) spiral in and are absorbed at a climbing front of soul light, each one paying for a slice of the body, ring on the floor at the start, flash at the head. Then you can move and shoot. Server: `MatchServer.onCharacter` (attributes `Revealing`, `RevealStart`); visuals: `RespawnReveal` (client). |
| **SlowMo** | `SLOWMO_TIME` 2.6s | The moment the wall reaches a finish line everything slows down to `SLOWMO_SCALE` 0.1 (eased in over `SLOWMO_RAMP` 0.5s): walking, sliding, jumping and falling (gravity x scale squared), and shooting (fire rate). The screen desaturates, cinematic bars slide in, short white hit. Implemented by `TimeScale` (shared module) read by `Movement`, `GunClient`, `MatchServer` (gravity) and `MatchFlow` (look). |
| **Result** | `RESULT_TIME` 5s | Cutscene: the **winning team** (up to 3) **sits on stone thrones** with glowing team-color trim under light columns (the middle one is raised), slow camera push-in, no confetti, "RED TEAM WINS" + VICTORY / DEFEAT for you. |
| **Summary** | each player until CONTINUE (client auto-presses 15s after the soul count; server gives up after `SUMMARY_MAX` 60s) | **Souls screen.** Your character stands still on the **left** (v3.7: no hopping, no idle moves); the stuff happens in the **middle**: MATCH BASE, then one row at a time pops in (small text): VICTORY/DEFEAT/DRAW, KILLS, BOXES PLUGGED, BOXES DESTROYED, DAMAGE (each with its count and its multiplier), then TOTAL MULTIPLIER, then the **soul number counts up rapidly from 1** to what you earned, then **CONTINUE** appears next to it and a **15s timer** starts (RETURNING TO LOBBY IN n). **v3.8: an XP bar sits at the very bottom** (LV n, +xp, bar): it fills a bit with every stat row that pops in and tops up at the total; level-ups flash gold. **v3.8: the mouse cursor is visible on the Pregame + Summary screens and clicks no longer fire your gun** (`MatchFlow` sets `GunMenuOpen`, `GunClient` shows the cursor and ignores input). Pressing it (or the timer ending) saves your profile and teleports you to the lobby (`Continue` remote -> `MatchRoster.SendOne`). |

**Souls formula** (`MatchConfig.REWARD`, computed on the server in `MatchServer.buildReward`, the client only displays it): `Base 50` x set multiplier (**Win 1.5 / Loss 0.75 / Draw 1.0**) x kills (`1 + 0.10 per kill`) x boxes plugged (`1 + 0.05 each`) x boxes destroyed (`1 + 0.08 each`) x damage (`1 + 0.02 per full 1000 damage` dealt to players). Each multiplier is rounded to 2 decimals before it is multiplied in, so what is shown multiplies to exactly the total. Souls go into the profile (`Souls`), Wins/Losses are counted as before. `PlaceConfig.SOULS_WIN / SOULS_LOSS` are no longer used.
**Stats** are Player attributes, reset every round by `MatchServer`: `StatKills` + `StatDamage` (added by `GunServer`, players only, damage capped at the target's remaining health), `StatPlugged` (whoever's carry/throw plugged the box) and `StatDestroyed` (last shooter when a plugged box is shot to 0; drained boxes give nobody credit).
**Mouse:** the game locks first person, so the interactive screens (Pregame, Summary) contain an invisible `Modal` button, which frees the cursor while they are open.

### 3.3 Special movement abilities (v3.8: REAL, one per loadout, MOVE slot)
Everyone keeps the base kit (sprint, crouch, slide, slide-jump, mantle). The MOVE slot holds ONE of these (script: `AbilityClient`, a small icon + cooldown / bar HUD sits bottom middle; all numbers in its `CFG`):
- **Double Jump** (icon: two chevrons): press SPACE again in the air for a second jump (6.8 studs). **Cooldown 3.5s.**
- **Mega Jump** (icon: big arrow over a coil): HOLD SPACE on the ground, a **charge bar** shows bottom middle (1.3s to full), release = launch 16 to 54 studs depending on the charge. A quick tap is a normal jump. You walk at half speed while charging. **Cooldown 6s.**
- **Wall Run** (icon: wall + speed lines): jump at a wall and hold a move key: you run along it in **any direction** (camera-relative, so looking up = running up), speed 30. **Walls only, never ceilings.** A **stamina bar** drains while running (3.2s from full) and refills on the ground; run dry and you drop and must regain 25% before the next run. SPACE = wall jump. **You can shoot while wall-running.** No cooldown (the stamina balances it).
- **Wall Climb** (icon: wall + ceiling corner + arrow): press SPACE next to a wall or under a ceiling and you stick to it (spider-man) **for as long as you want**, WASD moves you along it (camera-relative, over edges, onto ceilings). SPACE = jump off, C / Ctrl = let go. **Letting go starts a 3.5s cooldown. You CANNOT shoot while climbing** (gun is put away; `GunClient` hides it and `GunServer` refuses shots while the character attribute `AbilityMode == "climb"`).
- The old placeholders (Phase Shift, Glider, Wall Peek) are gone from `LobbyCatalog`. Old saves with one of them just show an empty MOVE slot.
- `AbilityClient` sets the character attribute `AbilityMode` (`wallrun` / `climb`); `Movement` stands down while it is set; `AbilityServer` validates it against the saved loadout and relays it. Both games have the same two scripts, so the abilities also work in the lobby.

### 5.4 Skins (built v3.1)
- `ReplicatedStorage/Skins` (ModuleScript, **both games**, identical copies). 3 starter skins, all free for now: **OPERATOR** (default:
  steel plates, amber visor), **VANDAL** (black hood, white mask with a red slash), **SALVAGER** (canvas, round goggles, scrap backpack).
- Equipped skin = profile key `Skin` (default `OPERATOR`, so a player who never played has the default on). Mirrored to the Player
  attribute `Skin`. `AvatarService` (both games) dresses your real character in it: you wear it walking around the lobby and in the match
  (in a match the glowing parts take the team color). The lobby stage + skins tab show it with `Skins.NewFigure`.
- **Body proportions (v3.2):** every player (real character + lobby figure) is **taller and skinnier** than the stock R15 rig: `Skins.Proportions = { Height = 1.12, Width = 0.84, Depth = 0.88, Head = 0.95 }`.
  Applied through the Humanoid scale values (`Skins.ApplyProportions`); `Skins.Prepare(char)` waits until the rig has really resized, and both
  `AvatarService` (skin gear) and `ThirdPersonServer` (world gun + IK) call it first so their sizes come from the final body. Tune the four numbers
  in `Skins.lua` (both games, identical copies). The skin gear is sized from the body parts, so it follows automatically.
- **Figure idle moves (v3.7):** the random moves (aim in, inspect, low ready, scan, turn, shift weight) only run when a figure is made with `opts.idle = true`, which only the **lobby squad stage** does. Pre-game, summary, skin catalog and cutscene figures just breathe. They are rarer: first move after 5-9 s, then 9-16 s between moves (`IDLE_FIRST` / `IDLE_GAP` in `Skins`). The gun poses live in `Skins.GunPose` and were tuned with an offline geometry check so the gun no longer sinks into the body (inspect used to bury ~0.6 studs of gun in the torso; low ready was worse). The figure gun is smaller (`GUN_S` 1.6 -> 1.25).
- Lobby skins (v3.4): the round **hanger button** left of PLAY (play screen and home HUD), or **TAB**, opens the **SKIN CATALOG** over the stage with the 3 skin cards; click = equip (`SetSkin` remote, validated in `LobbyServer`). The OUTFIT slot and the TAB SKINS hint are removed.
- Adding a skin: new entry in `Skins.List`, plus its gear in `DRESS[id]` (real character) and `FIGURE[id]` (menu figure). `SkinsOwned` is reserved for the shop.

### 5.4b Player level + XP (v3.8)
- Saved in the shared profile as `XP`; `ProfileStore` derives the Player attribute `Level` from it (`ReplicatedStorage.Levels`: level n needs `100 + 40*(n-1)` XP, max level 100).
- **Earned at the end of a Breakline match** (`MatchConfig.XP`, computed in `MatchServer.buildReward`, shown by `MatchFlow`): Base 40 + outcome (Win 60 / Draw 30 / Loss 20) + 12 per kill + 8 per box plugged + 10 per box destroyed + 2 per 100 damage. The breakdown is not shown, only the total on the XP bar.
- **Lobby:** a nameplate above every player's head (yours too): `[LV n] NAME` (`LobbyNameplates`).

## 7. Weapons

### 7.1 System (data-driven)
- **`WeaponConfig`** (ReplicatedStorage) — every gun's stats. Shared defaults in `W.Defaults`; each gun only lists what differs.
  Supports `FireMode` = Auto / Semi / Burst, `Pellets` (shotguns), `Cycle` = Bolt / Pump / Slide (animated + blocks firing),
  per-gun recoil pattern, spread, ADS speed/zoom, move-speed multiplier, equip time, shell look, fire-sound pitch/volume.
  Helpers: `W.Get(id)`, `W.TTK(cfg, head)`, `W.AvgInterval(cfg)`.
- **`GunModels`** (ReplicatedStorage) — every gun's model is built from parts in code, plus the **sights**. **v3.7:** every gun (with every sight it accepts) is one connected piece - checked offline with a box-overlap test (fixed: WASP grip + mag floated under the receiver, AK / ANVIL / LONGSHOT / HALO-50 / BREACHER stocks and guards had gaps, M4 buffer tube + charging handle, ANVIL bipod + rear sight, 8x scope rings). FAMAS is slimmer and shorter. Sizes: first-person `VM_SCALE` 0.78 (`GunClient`), world gun `GUN_SCALE` 0.8 (`ThirdPersonServer`), lobby figure `GUN_S` 1.25 (`Skins`). One description
  builds both the first-person viewmodel and the third-person world gun. *(Replaces the old GunModelBuilder + RifleModel.)*
- **`GunClient`** — one script runs every gun (fire modes, pellets, bolt/pump anims, optics, sniper scope overlay). Rebuilds instantly on swap.
- **`GunServer`** — validates every shot per weapon (fire rate incl. bursts, ammo, pellets, range, line of sight). Shotgun pellets
  are summed into **one** damage number per target. Owns the equipped gun as Player attributes `Weapon` + `Sight` (remote `SetLoadout`).
- **`ThirdPersonServer/Client`** — world gun rebuilds when the player swaps; IK hands follow the new grip points.
- Health = 100. Hitscan for all guns (for now).

### 7.2 Adding a new gun (checklist)
1. `WeaponConfig`: add `W.Weapons.MYGUN = { Name, Class, Inspiration, Sights, DefaultSight, ...stats }` and add `"MYGUN"` to `W.Order`.
2. `GunModels`: add `Models.MYGUN = function(k) ... end` using the kit (`k.box`, `k.cylZ`, `k.ring`, `k.glow`…). It **must** call
   `k.grip`, `k.fore`, `k.magwell`, `k.muzzle`, `k.eject`, `k.mount`, `k.irons`. Iron-sight parts go after `k.tag = "Iron"`.
   Mag parts after `k.group = "mag"`, bolt/pump/slide after `k.group = "cycle"`. Or reuse a model: `Model = "VX9"` in the config.
3. Done — it shows up in the Armory picker automatically with its stats and TTK.

## 8. Movement (base kit, everyone has it)
Goal: fast-paced and fluid. All speeds are multiplied by the gun's move-speed (7.4).
- **Walk 16 / sprint 30 / crouch 9** (sprint was 24). Speeds live at the top of `GunClient`.
- **Sprint:** hold Shift. **Mobile:** "Sprint" button above the jump button; **gamepad:** click the left stick. Both are tap-to-toggle
  (button turns accent-colored while on) and switch off when you stop moving. Sent to `Movement` as the `SprintToggle` attribute.
- **Jump (momentum):** low and snappy, not springy — jump height 4.6 (Roblox default 7.2) and 1.4× gravity on the way down only.
  You keep your run speed in the air, so a sprint-jump goes far and flat (+2.5 forward speed when jumping at ≥ 24 speed).
  Air control: steer slowly (turn rate 3.2 rad/s), pressing backwards brakes, no input = keep drifting. Hitting a wall drops the
  momentum into it. Walking off a ledge works the same way. All in `CFG` at the top of `Movement` (JUMP_HEIGHT, FALL_GRAVITY_MULT, AIR_*).
- **Crouch:** C / LeftCtrl / gamepad B / mobile button while **not** sprinting. Hold to crouch (`CROUCH_TOGGLE` in `Movement` switches
  to tap-toggle). Lowers hitbox + camera, can't sprint while crouched, pressing sprint stands you up. Stays crouched under low ceilings.
  Replicated as the `Crouching` character attribute (third person shows bent legs + lean).
- **Slide:** same key while sprinting (speed ≥ 20). Small start boost (+6), steering, lowered hitbox + camera, buffered input
  (press just before landing or while still speeding up). **Momentum rules:**
  - Flat ground (under ~3.5°): keeps **all** momentum for ~0.95s, then blends smoothly back into a run (or crouch if C is still held).
  - Downhill: **gains** speed (scaled by steepness, cap 58) and the slide timer pauses, so long ramps = long fast slides.
  - Uphill: **loses** speed slowly (scaled by steepness); ends early if it drops below 14.
  - Sliding off a ledge keeps the momentum into the air.
- **Slide-jump:** keeps 95% of momentum (also works in the short blend right after a slide ends).
- **Mantle:** jump into a ledge (1.4–7.5 studs). With the lower jump, mantle is how you get onto anything above ~4.5 studs.
- **First person:** your own body + gear are always hidden locally (fixes seeing your body while falling).
- **Speed feel (subtle, tunable in `SPEEDFX` at the top of `GunClient`):** sprint FOV 82 (was 80), slide FOV +7, extra FOV when going
  faster than a sprint (downhill slides); quick FOV kick + dip when a slide starts; light camera rumble while sliding; faint speed lines
  at the screen edges — start at ~17 speed, about 60% strength at a normal sprint, full at ~38 (slides) — plus a soft dark edge
  vignette at speed; all hidden while aiming. Optional looping wind sound (`Wind` in SoundBank — empty = silent).

- **Movement abilities (v3.8):** see section 3.3. `Movement` stands down (releases the slide / mantle / crouch) while `AbilityMode` is set; the abilities are only active when the matching one is in your MOVE slot.

## 9. Tech Principles
- **Data-driven:** stats in config modules (WeaponConfig, etc.).
- **Server-authoritative** for damage, ammo, fire rate; client does visuals and instant feedback.
- Damage numbers are shown from the **server-confirmed** value (HitConfirm).
- Parts players and bullets should pass through (e.g. the middle wall) need **CanCollide = false AND CanQuery = false**.
- **Wall Push map:** you build the map; `MatchServer` builds the gameplay pieces into `workspace.WallPush` at server start, fitted to your **`MapBounds`** part (anchored, transparent, bottom = ground; see 2.1). Tuning for fitting is in `MatchConfig.MAP`.
- **Box look:** `BoxModel` (ReplicatedStorage) builds the box and animates the faces from box attributes (`State`, `Health`, `MaxHealth`, `PlugTeam`, `Draining`); `MatchClient` runs it.
- **Carrying:** Player attribute `Carrying` (bool). Any new gun, gadget or ability script **must refuse to work while it is true** (GunClient + GunServer already do).
- **Box damage:** `GunServer` validates a shot on a plugged box and fires the `ServerStorage.BoxDamage` BindableEvent (part, damage, shooter); `MatchServer` applies it.
